Welcome to Nimbus Notes. This guide walks you through your first month. Nimbus Notes is a small team, so most of what you need is written down here or can be answered in the #eng-help channel.

## Before day one

Your laptop is shipped within 2 business days of your signed offer. If it has not arrived by your start date, tell your hiring manager and the IT desk. You will receive an invitation to the company password manager by email; accept it before your first day so that your other accounts can be shared with you.

## Day one

Orientation starts at 10:00 Asia/Taipei in the meeting room called Cirrus. In the morning you will be added to the GitHub organization nimbus-notes, to the NimbusVPN client, and to the team calendar. In the afternoon you meet your onboarding buddy, a more experienced engineer who answers questions during your first month and reviews your first pull requests.

## Your first week

By the end of your first week you should have a pull request merged. It can be small, for example a documentation fix or a test. The goal is to walk through the complete path from branch to production once, with your buddy beside you. Read the code review policy before you open your first pull request, because it describes how many approvals you need and how large a pull request may be.

## Your first 30 days

You will shadow one full on-call rotation before you join the rotation yourself. Shadowing means you receive the same pages as the primary engineer but you are not responsible for acting. You must also complete the mandatory security awareness training within 14 days of your start date. Training reminders are sent automatically on day 7 and day 12.
