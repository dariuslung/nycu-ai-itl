Nimbus Notes runs a weekly on-call rotation with a primary and a secondary engineer.

## Rotation

The rotation changes every Monday at 10:00 Asia/Taipei. The outgoing primary writes a short handoff note listing open incidents, noisy alerts, and anything that looks risky for the week. The incoming primary reads it before the handoff meeting.

## Eligibility

Engineers join the rotation after they have shadowed one full rotation (see the onboarding guide). Managers do not carry a pager, but they are available to the incident commander for decisions.

## Escalation

If the primary engineer has not acknowledged a page after 5 minutes, the paging system alerts the secondary engineer automatically. If neither responds, the on-call lead is called.

## Compensation

An engineer who is paged overnight for a SEV1 incident receives one compensatory day off, taken within the following two weeks.

## Swapping

Swaps are allowed if both engineers agree and the swap is recorded in the on-call calendar at least 24 hours in advance.
