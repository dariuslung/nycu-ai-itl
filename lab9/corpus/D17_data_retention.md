This policy states how long we keep different kinds of data.

## User content

When a user deletes a note, it moves to the trash and stays there for 30 days. After that it is removed permanently from the primary database. Backups that still contain the note are kept for 35 days, so the data disappears from backups at most 35 days after it leaves the trash.

## Accounts

When a user deletes an account there is a grace period of 7 days in which the deletion can be undone. After the grace period the account and its content are deleted.

## Logs and analytics

Application logs are kept for 90 days. Analytics events are kept for 13 months and then aggregated.

## Privacy requests

A request to export or delete personal data must be completed within 30 days of the verified request. The support team opens a ticket for the privacy contact on the day the request arrives.
