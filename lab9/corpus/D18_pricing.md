Nimbus Notes has three plans.

## Free

The Free plan includes 100 MB of storage and sync on up to 2 devices. It has no sharing of folders.

## Plus

The Plus plan costs US$4 per month and includes 10 GB of storage, unlimited devices, and folder sharing with up to 5 people.

## Team

The Team plan costs US$9 per user per month. Each user receives 100 GB of storage. It adds admin controls, usage reports, and a shared billing portal.

## Billing

Annual billing gives two months free compared with paying monthly. Prices are in US dollars. Taxes are added at checkout where required.
