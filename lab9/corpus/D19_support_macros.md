This handbook is for the support team.

## Support hours

Support is available on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 Asia/Taipei. Messages that arrive outside these hours are answered on the next business day. Our target for a first response is 4 business hours.

## Refunds

Customers may request a refund within 14 days of purchase. After 14 days, only annual plans in exceptional cases are refunded, and the decision belongs to the support lead. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method.

## Escalating to engineering

When a ticket looks like a bug, copy the ticket link into the #support-eng channel and add the app version. If the customer cannot sync, ask for the device type and the time of the last successful sync before you escalate.

## Tone

Be concrete and kind. Do not promise dates for fixes. Link to the status page when an incident is open.
