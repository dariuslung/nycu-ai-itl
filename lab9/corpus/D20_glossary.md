Short definitions of terms and abbreviations used in our documents.

## A to C

ADR: Architecture Decision Record, a short document that records one significant technical decision, with its context and consequences.

Canary: a stage of a release in which only a small share of traffic receives the new version.

CRB: Change Review Board. A group that meets on Thursdays to review database schema migrations and vendor changes.

## N to S

NSS: Nimbus Sync Service, another name for the sync-service.

Postmortem: a blameless written review of an incident.

SEV: severity. SEV1 is the most serious level, SEV3 the least.

## T to Z

Steward: see code steward. The code steward is the designated pull request reviewer for a period of time; the rotation is recorded in DECISIONS.md.

TLS: Transport Layer Security, the protocol that protects connections between services and between clients and the gateway.
